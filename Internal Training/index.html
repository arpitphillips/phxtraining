What We Believe

The core values and commitments that define how PHX India operates — and how you are expected to operate.

PHX India exists to deliver exceptional imaging work with integrity. Every decision, interaction, and deliverable must reflect these values. They are not aspirational — they are operational standards.

Quality First

Every deliverable must meet the highest possible standard. There is no acceptable reason to deliver substandard work.

Integrity Always

Be honest with clients, with colleagues, and with the firm. Do not overpromise. Do not conceal problems.

Professionalism

Conduct yourself with dignity, punctuality, and respect in every situation — internally and externally.

Continuous Improvement

We seek to improve with every project. Feedback is welcomed. Stagnation is not acceptable.

These values govern your daily decisions If you are uncertain about a course of action, ask yourself whether it reflects quality, integrity, and professionalism. If the answer is no — do not proceed without consulting your manager.

Our Philosophy

How PHX India approaches its work, its clients, and its people.

PHX India does not compete on price. We compete on the quality and consistency of our output, and on the trust we build with every client relationship. Our philosophy is simple: relentless standards, every time, without exception.

How We Work

  • Clients receive one point of contact — the CRM Team. All other teams work behind this interface, never around it.
  • Every project follows the same 12-stage lifecycle. There are no shortcuts. Skipping a stage creates risk — and the person who skipped it is accountable.
  • Problems are escalated immediately, not managed privately. An undisclosed problem is worse than the problem itself.
  • Client feedback — including CSS Feedback — is mandatory. It is how we measure ourselves and improve.
  • Every employee is a representative of the firm. How you behave on-site, online, and in conversation reflects PHX India.
The PHX India Standard If it is worth doing, it is worth doing properly. This is not a slogan — it is an operational requirement. Hold yourself and your colleagues to this standard every day.

Code of Ethics

The ethical principles every PHX India employee must uphold — without exception.

PHX India's reputation depends entirely on the conduct of its people. The following principles are non-negotiable. Any breach is treated seriously and may result in disciplinary action or termination.

Core Principles

  • Confidentiality. Client information, project details, and firm data are strictly confidential. Do not share them externally — not verbally, not digitally, not on social media.
  • Honesty. Do not misrepresent your work, your attendance, your expenses, or your conduct. Dishonesty — even once — breaks trust permanently.
  • Respect. Treat every colleague, client, and vendor with respect regardless of their role or seniority. Harassment, discrimination, and intimidation are grounds for immediate dismissal.
  • No conflicts of interest. Do not take on work, clients, or business arrangements that compete with or compromise PHX India. Disclose any potential conflict immediately.
  • Report violations. If you witness behaviour that violates these principles, you have an obligation to report it. Silence is complicity.
Zero Tolerance Theft, harassment, falsification of records, and sharing confidential client data without authorisation are zero-tolerance violations. They result in immediate dismissal without notice.

General Conduct

The day-to-day behavioural standards expected of every employee at PHX India.

Every employee is expected to conduct themselves in a manner that reflects positively on PHX India at all times — inside the office, on location, and in any context where they represent the firm.

Workplace Standards

  • Arrive on time. Punctuality is a basic professional standard, not a courtesy.
  • Mark attendance accurately. Do not ask others to mark attendance on your behalf.
  • Keep your workspace clean and organised. Shared spaces must be left as you found them.
  • Dress appropriately for the work environment and any client-facing occasions.
  • Use respectful language at all times. Profanity and aggressive communication are not acceptable.
  • Personal grievances between colleagues must be addressed privately and escalated through the proper channel if unresolved — not aired publicly or in group chats.

Prohibited Behaviour

  • Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any colleague, client, or vendor.
  • Consuming alcohol or any controlled substance on firm premises or during working hours.
  • Using firm resources — including equipment, internet, or time — for personal commercial activity.
  • Spreading rumours, misinformation, or negative commentary about the firm, clients, or colleagues.

Professional Conduct

Standards for client interactions, communication, and representation of PHX India externally.

The way PHX India is perceived externally is entirely shaped by the conduct of its people. Every client interaction — however brief — either builds or erodes trust. These standards are not optional.

Client Communication

  • Only the CRM Team communicates directly with clients. Production and Studios must not contact clients independently unless explicitly authorised.
  • All communication must be accurate, timely, and professional in tone. Casual or ambiguous communication leads to misunderstandings that damage client relationships.
  • Never make commitments or promises to a client that you are not authorised to make. If in doubt, say "I will confirm and get back to you" — then do so promptly.
  • Do not discuss pricing, scope changes, or complaints on behalf of the firm without CRM involvement.

On-Location Conduct

  • Arrive prepared. Review the brief fully before leaving the office.
  • Be respectful of the client's property, staff, and guests.
  • Do not photograph, film, or share images from a client's location without explicit permission.
  • If issues arise on location that were not in the brief, contact CRM immediately — do not improvise solutions with the client.
You represent PHX India At all times — on location, in transit, and on social media — you carry the firm's reputation with you. Conduct yourself accordingly.

Expenses & Reimbursements

How to claim work-related expenses and the timeline for reimbursement.

PHX India reimburses legitimate work-related expenses. Claims must be submitted promptly, accurately, and with supporting documentation. Expenses claimed without receipts or approval will not be processed.

7
Days to reimburse after voucher submission

How to Claim

  • Retain original receipts for every expense you intend to claim. Digital photos of receipts are acceptable only if pre-approved by your manager.
  • Complete the expense voucher in full — date, amount, purpose, project reference, and your signature.
  • Submit the voucher to accounts within the same week the expense was incurred, wherever possible.
  • Reimbursement will be processed within 7 working days of receipt of a complete, approved voucher.

What Is Not Reimbursable

  • Personal expenses — meals, travel, or purchases not directly required for firm business.
  • Expenses incurred without prior approval where approval was required.
  • Claims submitted without receipts or supporting documentation.
  • Expenses claimed more than 30 days after they were incurred without a valid reason for the delay.

Firm Assets

The rules governing your use of PHX India equipment, property, and resources.

PHX India's equipment — cameras, lenses, lighting, computers, vehicles, and office property — is a significant investment. Every employee who uses firm assets is personally responsible for their appropriate use and safe return.

Usage Policy

  • Firm equipment is provided for firm business only. Using it for personal projects, freelance work, or any other non-PHX India purpose is prohibited.
  • Equipment must be checked out and checked back in through the proper asset log. Never take equipment home overnight without explicit approval.
  • Report any fault, damage, or loss to your manager immediately. Do not attempt to repair equipment yourself.
  • Storage and charging of equipment is your responsibility on the day it is assigned to you.

Damage & Loss

Negligence is a personal liability If firm equipment is damaged or lost due to negligence — careless handling, failure to secure, or improper storage — the responsible employee may be required to contribute to the cost of repair or replacement.
  • Accidental damage during normal use is treated differently from damage caused by negligence. Always report damage honestly and promptly.
  • Lost equipment must be reported to management on the same day it is discovered missing.

Conflict of Interest

What constitutes a conflict of interest and what you must do if one arises.

A conflict of interest arises when your personal, financial, or professional interests could — or could be seen to — interfere with your ability to act in the best interests of PHX India and its clients. Even the appearance of a conflict must be taken seriously.

Common Conflicts

  • Running a competing imaging, photography, video, or styling business while employed at PHX India.
  • Taking on freelance work for a current or recent PHX India client without management approval.
  • Having a financial interest in a vendor, supplier, or client that could influence your professional decisions.
  • Recommending a vendor, supplier, or contractor for personal gain.

Your Obligation

Disclose immediately If you believe you have — or may have — a conflict of interest, you must disclose it to management immediately. Failure to disclose is treated as a serious breach of trust and may result in dismissal. You will not be penalised for disclosing in good faith.

Client relationships developed through your work at PHX India are firm relationships — not personal ones. You may not solicit or redirect clients for personal business during your employment or for a reasonable period after leaving the firm.

Digital & Social Media Ethics

The rules governing your use of digital platforms, firm data, and client content.

In the imaging industry, digital content is the product. Mishandling it — even inadvertently — can cause significant harm to clients and to PHX India's reputation. These rules are strict and apply to all employees at all times.

Client Content

  • Client work — photographs, videos, raw files, edited deliverables — is the property of the client. Do not share, post, or distribute it without explicit written authorisation from both the client and PHX India management.
  • Do not use client work in your personal portfolio without written approval from the client and the firm.
  • Never share raw or unedited files with anyone outside the firm — not even with the client.

Social Media

  • Do not post about clients, projects, or firm matters on personal social media without approval.
  • Do not tag PHX India locations, clients, or staff in posts that could be embarrassing or reputationally damaging.
  • Any social media activity that publicly criticises the firm, its clients, or its staff may result in disciplinary action.

Data & Devices

  • Firm data — contact lists, client records, project files, financial information — is confidential and must not be copied, transferred, or accessed outside the firm without authorisation.
  • Do not install unauthorised software on firm computers or devices.
  • On your last day of employment, all firm data and access credentials must be returned or handed over. Retaining firm data after leaving is a legal violation.

Code Violations & Discipline

How PHX India handles violations of policy and standards — and what each level means for you.

PHX India operates a structured disciplinary process for violations that are not grounds for immediate dismissal. The process is fair, documented, and escalating. The goal is correction — not punishment — except in cases of gross misconduct.

Disciplinary Process

LevelTypeDescription
1Verbal WarningFirst instance of a minor violation. Conversation with your manager. Recorded internally.
2Written WarningRepeated or more serious violation. Formal written record. Copy kept on file.
3Final Written WarningFurther violation after written warning. States that dismissal will follow if conduct does not improve.
4DismissalContinued violations after final warning — or a single act of gross misconduct.

Gross Misconduct — Immediate Dismissal

The following result in immediate dismissal without notice
  • Theft of firm property, client property, or a colleague's property.
  • Physical assault, harassment, or threats directed at any person.
  • Sharing confidential client data externally without authorisation.
  • Falsification of attendance, expenses, or any firm record.
  • Being under the influence of alcohol or controlled substances during work hours.
  • Serious insubordination — deliberate and repeated refusal to carry out reasonable instructions from management.

Cell Phone Policy

The rules on personal phone use during work hours and on firm premises.

Personal phone use during work hours is permitted only within reasonable limits. Excessive personal phone use affects productivity and professionalism — especially in client-facing situations.

Usage Rules

  • Personal phone calls should be limited to breaks and genuine emergencies. Step away from your workspace for personal calls.
  • Do not use your phone during meetings, client interactions, or active shoot assignments.
  • Do not use your phone to photograph or record inside the office or on client premises without explicit approval.
  • Phones must be on silent or vibrate during office hours. Loud ringtones or speaker calls in shared spaces are not acceptable.

Photography & Recording

No photography of the workplace without approval This includes the office, studio spaces, equipment, colleagues, and client locations. Unauthorized photography is a breach of privacy and may also violate client confidentiality agreements.
  • Any images taken on personal devices during firm assignments remain subject to the firm's confidentiality rules. Do not post them without authorisation.
  • If a client asks you to photograph or share content, refer them to CRM. Do not act on such requests independently.

    Policies & Rules

    Company-wide operational policies. These are hard rules governing payment, communication, scope management, and file handling. No exceptions unless explicitly authorised by senior management.

    Advance Payment Policy Hard Rule

    50% advance is required before confirming any Photography, Videography, or Reel assignment. 60% advance is required before confirming any Styling assignment. No shoot may be confirmed until the advance is received in full. CRM is responsible for collecting and confirming receipt before the Production Team is briefed. There are no exceptions to this policy.

    Balance Payment Policy Hard Rule

    The remaining balance must be received before or alongside Final Delivery. Final deliverables must not be released until CRM confirms that the balance payment has been received. No exceptions.

    Scope Changes Important

    Any deliverable changes during or after production may incur additional charges. If the client requests changes to the originally agreed scope during the shoot, the Production Team must inform them that additional charges may apply and immediately escalate to CRM. CRM must communicate any cost implications to the client before the changes are actioned.

    Location & Access Important

    The client is responsible for providing location passes, entry clearances, and access arrangements to CRM a minimum of 2 days before the shoot. PHX INDIA does not chase this — it is the client's responsibility. CRM must ensure this information is received and relayed to the Production Team.

    Client Communication Hard Rule

    All client communication flows exclusively through the CRM Team. The Production Team and Editing Studios do not interact with clients directly — not by phone, email, message, or in person outside of the shoot. If a client contacts Production or Studios directly, they must be politely redirected to CRM.

    Raw Files Hard Rule

    Raw, unedited files are never shared with clients under any circumstances. Only final, edited deliverables are released. There are no exceptions to this policy, regardless of client relationship or request.

    Deliverable Changes Important

    Changes to agreed deliverables during production are potentially chargeable and must be escalated to CRM immediately. The Production Team must not agree to, reject, or negotiate changes independently.

    Policies Pending Finalisation

    The following policies are under review and will be added once approved by management:

    • Post-production turnaround times by project type
    • Revision policy — number of included rounds and charges for additional rounds
