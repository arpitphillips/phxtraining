Dos & Don'ts

Clear operational guidelines for every team. These are not suggestions — they are standards that every employee must follow without exception.

CRM Team

CRM Team

Do

  • Always confirm advance payment before briefing the Production Team
  • Ensure Scope of Work is 100% complete before sending an Estimate
  • Share location passes and clearances with Production at least 2 days before the shoot
  • Be the sole point of client communication at all times
  • Follow up for CSS Feedback on every single project — no exceptions
  • Brief the Production Team fully before every shoot

Don't

  • Never confirm a shoot without the advance payment received
  • Never allow the client to communicate directly with the Production Team or Studios
  • Never send a Final Delivery before balance payment is confirmed
  • Never skip the CSS Feedback stage
Production

Production Team

Do

  • Ensure you are fully briefed before arriving on shoot day
  • Ask the client to share their vision at the START of the shoot
  • Communicate any deliverable changes immediately to CRM — not directly to the client
  • Hand all raw material to Editing Studios promptly after the shoot

Don't

  • Never make promises to the client about deliverables — that is CRM's role
  • Never discuss pricing or changes to scope with the client
  • Never start a shoot without confirmation that advance has been received
  • Never communicate with the client independently — always through CRM
Studios

Editing Studios

Do

  • Action all feedback communicated through CRM promptly
  • Coordinate Final Delivery confirmation with CRM before sending
  • Maintain organised file management for every project

Don't

  • Never release final files without CRM confirming balance payment is received
  • Never communicate directly with the client about feedback or changes
  • Never share raw or unedited files under any circumstances

Policies & Rules

Company-wide operational policies. These are hard rules governing payment, communication, scope management, and file handling. No exceptions unless explicitly authorised by senior management.

Advance Payment Policy Hard Rule

50% advance is required before confirming any Photography, Videography, or Reel assignment. 60% advance is required before confirming any Styling assignment. No shoot may be confirmed until the advance is received in full. CRM is responsible for collecting and confirming receipt before the Production Team is briefed. There are no exceptions to this policy.

Balance Payment Policy Hard Rule

The remaining balance must be received before or alongside Final Delivery. Final deliverables must not be released until CRM confirms that the balance payment has been received. No exceptions.

Scope Changes Important

Any deliverable changes during or after production may incur additional charges. If the client requests changes to the originally agreed scope during the shoot, the Production Team must inform them that additional charges may apply and immediately escalate to CRM. CRM must communicate any cost implications to the client before the changes are actioned.

Location & Access Important

The client is responsible for providing location passes, entry clearances, and access arrangements to CRM a minimum of 2 days before the shoot. PHX INDIA does not chase this — it is the client's responsibility. CRM must ensure this information is received and relayed to the Production Team.

Client Communication Hard Rule

All client communication flows exclusively through the CRM Team. The Production Team and Editing Studios do not interact with clients directly — not by phone, email, message, or in person outside of the shoot. If a client contacts Production or Studios directly, they must be politely redirected to CRM.

Raw Files Hard Rule

Raw, unedited files are never shared with clients under any circumstances. Only final, edited deliverables are released. There are no exceptions to this policy, regardless of client relationship or request.

Deliverable Changes Important

Changes to agreed deliverables during production are potentially chargeable and must be escalated to CRM immediately. The Production Team must not agree to, reject, or negotiate changes independently.
